        Sync your devices

        Keep your streak and results on your phone, tablet and computer. Your sync code is all it takes: no email, no password. Keep it to yourself, since anyone with it can load your progress.

        How to play

        Every day there are seven famous songs, each described as badly as we could manage, and a theme that links them. Type the song's title. You have 25 seconds for each, wrong guesses cost nothing, and your first right answer counts.

        Hints

        With 12 seconds left you get the decade. With 6 seconds left you get the title's first letter. Spelling doesn't need to be perfect, and you can add the artist if you like.

        The career ladder

        Every song you get moves you one step up, from busking outside the station to headlining Glastonbury.

          Sharing

          🟩 means you got it before any hints, 🟨 after a hint, and 🟥 means you missed it.

          The songs

          When each song ends, a 30-second preview plays from Apple Music, provided courtesy of iTunes. It streams straight from Apple, and you can turn it off with the Sound button.

          Privacy

          No accounts and no names. Your results are saved against a random ID that lives in your browser, and individual results are deleted after two weeks. Only the totals are kept.
