How it works

  • 12 rungs from busking to headlining. Every song you get moves you up one.
  • 25 seconds a song. The decade shows at 12 seconds, the first letter at 6.
  • Three misses and the band splits. Passing counts as a miss.
  • Fans: 100 a song, plus 50 with no hints and 25 for under 8 seconds. Cred: deep cuts earn it; knowing the really obvious ones costs it.
  • The higher you climb, the deeper the cuts. Dez from Second Pressings Records will let you know what he thinks.
  • Things happen on the road, and in the music papers. Each one goes in your Bootleg Archive.
  • The weeklies will call you the best new band in Britain. They say that a lot.

The Bootleg Archive

Legendary nights and famous flops, reviewed by Pitchblende (the only music site that matters, to itself), plus your front pages in New Musical Excess and Melody Faker. Collect them on the road.
